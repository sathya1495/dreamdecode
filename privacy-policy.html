Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Xgen Labs ("us", "we", or "our") operates the DreamDecode AI mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

DreamDecode AI is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.

All dream entries and journal data you create within the App are stored locally on your device only using on-device storage (SQLite). We do not have access to your dream entries or any content you create in the App.

2. AI Dream Interpretation

When you request a dream interpretation, the text of your dream is sent to a third-party AI service (Groq API) for processing. This data is used solely to generate your dream interpretation and is subject to Groq's Privacy Policy. We do not store or retain this data on our servers.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the App. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location data (country/region level)
  • App usage data

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use their services.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device's Settings > Google > Ads and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

4. In-App Purchases

Our App offers a premium subscription ("DreamDecode Pro") through Google Play In-App Payments. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses). Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how your payment data is handled.

5. Notifications

The App may send you local push notifications (such as daily dream journal reminders) if you enable this feature. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — for managing subscriptions (Privacy Policy)
  • Groq — for AI dream interpretation (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Services — for in-app purchases and app distribution (Privacy Policy)

7. Data Retention

Since all your dream data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete individual dreams within the App or delete all data by uninstalling the App. We retain no data on our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your data. Since dream journal data is stored locally on your device, its security is managed by your device's built-in security features (such as screen lock and encryption).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
