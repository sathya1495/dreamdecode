Delete Your Data — DreamDecode AI

How Your Data is Stored

DreamDecode AI stores all your dream journal entries, interpretations, and preferences locally on your device only. We do not store any of your personal data on our servers.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete Individual Dreams

Open the app → Go to your Journal → Tap on a dream → Tap the delete button to remove individual entries.

Option 2: Delete All App Data

On your Android device:

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → DreamDecode AI
  2. Tap "Storage & cache"
  3. Tap "Clear storage" to delete all app data

Option 3: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling DreamDecode AI will permanently delete all data associated with the app from your device.

Third-Party Data

If you wish to reset your advertising identifier (used by Google AdMob for serving ads):

  1. Go to Settings → Google → Ads
  2. Tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID"
